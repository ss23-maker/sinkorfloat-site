Sink or Float

Sink or Float Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Sink or Float game for Android (package name com.sportspheremedia.sinkorfloat) handles your information. The game is published on Google Play by Sportsphere Media Pty Ltd, a company registered in Australia (“we” or “us”).

Summary

Sink or Float does not collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The whole game is inside the app and works without an internet connection, and your progress stays on your phone.

What the game stores on your device

To save your place, the game keeps the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your progress, such as levels unlocked, stars, scores and streaks
  • your attempts and the time until the next one refills
  • which tips you have already seen, and how many shares you have made today

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your phone’s settings deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not send anything over the internet. Its pictures, films, music and fonts are all packed inside the app.
  • It does not use your phone’s location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Sharing

When you tap a share button, the game makes a picture card of your result and a short message on your device, then opens your phone’s share menu. You choose the app and the people it goes to, and it does not pass through us. Sharing earns an extra attempt, up to three a day. To give it, the game only learns that you picked an app in the share menu, not which app or who you sent it to.

Children

Sink or Float collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates of the game. Information Google collects when you use Google Play is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
